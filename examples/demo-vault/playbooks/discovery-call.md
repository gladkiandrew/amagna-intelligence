# Discovery call

Forty minutes, three parts:

1. **Ten minutes on the trigger.** What changed, and when. Do not pitch here.
2. **Twenty minutes on the five questions** in [[qualify-a-lead]].
3. **Ten minutes on what happens next**, with a date. A call that ends in "we'll be
   in touch" is a call that failed, regardless of how well it went.

If the floor comes up, quote it plainly — see [[pricing-floor]]. Do not soften it
and do not hint at flexibility you have not decided to offer.
