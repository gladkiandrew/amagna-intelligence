# Handover

The failure mode is delivery starting from the proposal instead of from the calls.
So the handover artefact is not the proposal — it is one page with:

- The trigger, in the client's own words.
- What they have already tried and what they blame for it failing.
- The number they flinched at, and the number they agreed to.
- The one outcome they will judge month three by.

If any of those four is unknown, the handover is not ready and the kickoff moves.
