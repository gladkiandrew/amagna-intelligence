# Qualifying a lead

Five questions, asked on the first call, in this order:

1. What made you look for this *now*? (No trigger, no urgency, no deal.)
2. Who signs? (If the answer is "we'd discuss it", see [[why-we-say-no]].)
3. What happens if you do nothing? (If the answer is "not much", the budget is not real.)
4. What have you already tried? (Tells you what you are replacing, and what they blame.)
5. What is the number you have in mind? (Ask it directly. The flinch is the information.)

A lead that answers all five clearly becomes a proposal the same week. A lead that
dodges two of them goes in the inbox with a date, not a proposal.
