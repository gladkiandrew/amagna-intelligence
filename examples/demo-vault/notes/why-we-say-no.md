# Why we say no

Four shapes we decline, in order of how much each has cost us historically:

1. **No single decision-maker.** If approval needs three people who disagree, the
   work gets re-litigated every month. Every engagement we lost money on had this.
2. **Scope defined by hours, not outcome.** "We want 20 hours a month" means nobody
   has agreed on what success is, and the review conversation never ends.
3. **Below the floor.** See [[pricing-floor]].
4. **Industry we have no notes on.** We can learn one new sector per quarter, not
   three. Saying yes to a fourth means doing all four badly.

Saying no early is cheap. Saying no in month three costs the margin on the whole
engagement plus the reference we would have had.
