# The pricing floor

The floor is $4,000 per month. It is not a negotiating position; it is the number
below which a client costs more to serve than they pay.

Two things we learned the expensive way:

- **Discounting to win a logo does not convert into a reference.** The two accounts
  we took at $2,800 in early 2025 both churned inside seven months, and neither
  agreed to a case study. Pattern: a client who negotiated hard on price negotiates
  hard on scope for the whole engagement.
- **Below the floor, delivery quality drops first and silently.** We staff thinner,
  review less, and the work gets worse before anyone complains.

So the rule: we quote the floor, and if the answer is no, the answer is no. Going
under it has to be a deliberate decision with a reason written down — not a
discount offered in a call to avoid an awkward pause.
