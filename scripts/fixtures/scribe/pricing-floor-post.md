We hold a price floor, and we tell people what it is.

Four thousand a month. Below that, an engagement costs more to serve than it pays —
not in theory, in our own numbers. Two accounts we took under the floor in early
2025 both churned inside seven months, and neither agreed to be a reference. The
pattern was the same in both: a client who negotiates hard on price negotiates hard
on scope for the whole engagement.

So the floor is not an opening position. If the answer is no, the answer is no, and
we would rather hear it in the first call than in month three.
